Chapter 03

The Workflow — Start to Post

Five steps take this from a blank screen to a posted reel. The whole thing is repeatable for any product — only the content changes, the system stays the same.

Generate 5 keyframes

In Nano Banana Pro at 2K, 9:16 — one frame per stage of the story (liquid → forming → fills + drop → stopper → hero). Lock the first one's look, then the rest inherit the same void, light and palette.

Animate each transition

One Kling 3.0 clip per transition with a START + END frame, 3s, pro, 1080p. The end frame of each clip is the start frame of the next — so the four clips chain together seamlessly.

Concat into the spot

Join the 4 clips in order with ffmpeg → one clean 12-second 1080p spot, 9:16. This is the finished commercial, silent.

Sound design

Generate the SFX + a soft ambient music bed, then place each sound frame-accurate on its beat and mix under a gentle limiter. Silent footage becomes a premium ad.

The "Cinematic AI Ad" reel layout

Render the storyboard + prompt overlay in HTML, composite the spot into it, and respect Instagram's safe-zones (title, buttons, caption). That's the post.

The Motion Prompts — How It Moves

Four Kling 3.0 clips, each with a START + END frame, 3 seconds, pro, 1080p, sound off. The end frame of every clip is the start frame of the next — so they chain seamlessly into the 12-second spot.

Clip 1 · KF1 → KF2 — Liquid Becomes Glass
The suspended ribbon of golden perfume slowly coalesces and gathers as a clear rectangular glass flacon forms around it. Camera slowly pushes in, then settles and holds. Warm golden light from upper left, drifting vapor. Smooth liquid-to-glass transformation. No warping, no flicker, stable picture.
Clip 2 · KF2 → KF3 — Fills With Gold
The glass stopper gently lifts away and floats up out of frame as the flacon fills from the base with luminous golden perfume, a single droplet forming above the open neck. Camera holds locked and steady. Warm golden light from upper left. No camera shake, no morphing of the glass, stable picture.
Clip 3 · KF3 → KF4 — Drop Falls, Stopper Descends
The droplet falls into the open neck as the faceted glass stopper descends from above toward the opening, condensation beading on the glass. Camera slowly dollies in toward the neck, then settles and holds. Warm golden light from upper left. No warping, no jitter, sharp label typography, stable picture.
Clip 4 · KF4 → KF5 — Seals + Hero Reveal
The faceted stopper settles onto the flacon and seats firmly, sealing the bottle. Camera slowly pulls back to reveal the full sealed CHANEL N°5 flacon, vapor drifting across the frame, then settles and holds. Warm golden light from upper left. No warping, no flicker, sharp label typography, stable picture.

The two rules that make it work: one camera move per clip, and always end with a closing phrase ("then settles and holds") so Kling doesn't hang on open-ended motion. Plus the quality suffix on every clip: 4K, Ultra HD, rich details, sharp clarity, cinematic texture, no blur, no ghosting, stable picture.

The Sound Design — What Sells the Morph

50–80% of reels are watched on mute — but the ones watched with sound feel ten times more expensive. Every effect is generated with ElevenLabs and dropped frame-accurate on its beat, under a soft music bed.

0s

Golden shimmer / whoosh

An airy, ethereal shimmer as the liquid gold swirls and coalesces in the void.

~2s

Liquid droplet

A soft, delicate drop with a gentle reverb tail as the form gathers.

~3s

Soft fill

A smooth, premium liquid flow as the flacon fills with gold.

~6s

Droplet into the neck

A single fine plink as the drop lands in the open bottle.

~9s

Crystal glass cling + sparkle

A refined glass chime on the seal, with a fine sparkle tail — the signature moment.

0–12s

Ambient luxury music bed

A soft warm pad under everything, faded in and out, kept low so the effects breathe.

Mix tip: push the effects to the front, keep the music quiet, and run the whole mix through a gentle limiter so nothing clips. The glass cling on the seal is the beat people remember.

Chapter 07

The Pitfalls — What Breaks It

Every one of these was found the hard way. None throw an error — they just look wrong, or quietly waste a generation.

01

Morph back-loads

Most of the liquid-to-glass transformation lands in the last half of the clip, leaving the first half feeling static.

02

Stopper continuity blink

The formed bottle already has a stopper, but the next stage shows an open neck — so the stopper appears to flicker between frames.

03

Preset hijack

Kling's auto preset suggestion silently overrides your custom prompt, and the result ignores everything you wrote.

04

End-frame freeze

A fixed end frame decelerates the entire scene onto that still — the world "parks" at the end of the clip.

05

Rigid glass warps

On a big reframe between start and end, the rigid bottle geometry morphs and "swims" instead of staying solid.

06

Label text drift

The "N°5" text on glass renders inconsistently across frames unless the typography is pinned in the prompt.

07

Flat, lifeless sound

Without mapping each effect to its exact beat, the audio feels generic and the morph stops feeling real.

08

Instagram safe-zones

Title and CTA placed too high or too low vanish behind the app's UI — header, buttons and caption.

The exact fixes — the prompt lines, the timings, the mix — aren't in this guide, by design. Knowing these eight exist is most of the fix. Each one cost real time on this build; that's the part no prompt gives you.
